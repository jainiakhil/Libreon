Libreon

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026. This covers the Libreon app for Android, from Google Play or GitHub, and this website.

The short version

Libreon collects nothing. It has no internet permission, so Android itself stops it from going online. Your documents stay on your phone.

Your documents

  • Libreon opens only the files you choose, through Android's own file picker or when you open a file with Libreon from another app.
  • To protect unsaved work, it keeps private copies of documents you're editing inside its own app storage on your phone. No other app can read them, and they never leave your phone.
  • Saved signatures, settings and the list of recent files are also stored only inside the app on your phone. Uninstalling Libreon removes all of it.

What Libreon does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no trackers.
  • No AI features, and nothing is sent anywhere to be processed.
  • No data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.

Links that open other apps

A few things open another app, and only when you tap them: links in your documents, the "Report a problem" link (GitHub, in your browser) and, in the GitHub version, the Buy Me a Coffee link. What happens there is covered by those apps' and websites' own privacy policies.

Optional tips (Google Play version)

The Google Play version lets you leave an optional tip. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play: Libreon never sees your payment details, and the developer sees only the standard order details Google Play reports to every seller. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Sharing

When you use Share, the document goes only to the app you pick in Android's share sheet.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers. It is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which, like any web host, handles the technical requests needed to deliver the page. A light or dark theme you choose is remembered only in your own browser.

Children

Libreon collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The source code is public on GitHub, so you can check all of this yourself. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
